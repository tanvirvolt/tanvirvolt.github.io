Motor Current Calculator

Estimate full-load line current from motor input power, supply voltage, efficiency and power factor. Enter motor output power in kW; the calculation estimates electrical current at the stated operating assumptions.

Formula

Three-phase: I = Pout × 1000 / (√3 × V × η × PF). Single-phase: I = Pout × 1000 / (V × η × PF). Here η is efficiency as a decimal and PF is power factor.

Important limitations

  • This is an estimate, not a substitute for the motor nameplate or manufacturer data.
  • Actual current varies with load, starting conditions, voltage and motor design.
  • Use applicable electrical codes and engineering judgement when selecting cables and protective devices.
