Cable Voltage Drop Calculator

Use this simple estimate to understand how conductor resistance contributes to voltage drop. It uses a simplified copper resistivity model and a round-trip length assumption for a two-conductor circuit.

Voltage drop: —

Formula used

Approximate resistance R = ρ × (2L) / A, with copper resistivity ρ ≈ 0.0175 Ω·mm²/m at a reference temperature. Voltage drop ΔV = I × R; percentage drop = ΔV / system voltage × 100.

Design limitations

  • This simplified model does not fully account for conductor temperature, AC reactance, power factor, installation method or parallel conductors.
  • For three-phase circuits and final cable selection, use an appropriate engineering method and applicable local standards.
  • Verify current-carrying capacity, protective-device coordination and permissible voltage-drop limits separately.
