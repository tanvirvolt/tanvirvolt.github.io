Power Factor Calculator

Calculate apparent power and reactive power when active power and power factor are known. This calculator is useful for learning basic AC power relationships.

Formula

Apparent power S = P / PF. Reactive power Q = √(S² − P²). P is active power in kW, S is apparent power in kVA, Q is reactive power in kVAR and PF is the power factor.

Notes

  • Power factor must be greater than zero and no greater than 1 for this simplified calculation.
  • Actual system measurements may vary with harmonics, unbalanced loads and operating conditions.
  • Power-factor correction equipment should be sized using proper load data and engineering review.
